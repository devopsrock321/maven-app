A maven is an expert or a person with deep knowledge and understanding of a particular subject.
General Meaning & Origin
• Definition: A trusted connoisseur or specialist whom people turn to for advice.
• Examples: A "fashion maven" or a "computer maven".
• Origin: The word comes from the Yiddish word meyvn and the Hebrew word mevin, which mean "one who understands".
• Synonyms: Expert, guru, master, virtuoso.
